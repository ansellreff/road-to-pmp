Guide for version 1.1 · 24 Nov 2026

This guide explains every part of Road to PMP. Each part says what it does, how to use it, its rules and limits,
and gives an example. The list of parts is on the right (on a phone: the **Contents** button at the bottom).

## Your study today

**What it does.** The home page shows where you are: the module you are in, the next step in it, how many
flashcards are due, and your path through the certifications.

**How to use it.**
1. Click **Road to PMP** at the top left to come back here from any page.
2. Press the button on the large card to continue where you stopped (the lesson, the quiz or the flashcards).
3. Under **Your certification path**, choose a certification to see all its modules.

**Rules and limits.**
- A certification is offered for study once at least one of its modules is written.
- The small chip beside the display buttons counts your study streak: days in a row on which you answered a
  question, rated a flashcard or finished a lesson.

**Example.** You finished the quiz of module 1.2 yesterday. Today the large card offers "Practice the flashcards"
of module 1.2.

## Modules: lesson, quiz and flashcards

**What it does.** Each module teaches one part of the exam in three steps: a lesson to read, a quiz to test
yourself, and flashcards to remember the key terms.

**How to use it.**
1. Open **Modules** in the top bar and choose a module.
2. Read the **Lesson**. The list on the right shows the sections; the one you are reading is marked. At the end,
   press **Mark lesson as completed**.
3. Open the **Quiz** tab. Answer each question and read the explanation. At the end you see your score and
   **Your answers**.
4. Open the **Flashcards** tab. Think of the answer, turn the card, and press **I know this** or **Still learning**.
5. The button at the bottom of each step leads to the next one, and after the flashcards to the next module.

**Rules and limits.**
- A lesson remembers the last section you reached and offers **Continue from** next time.
- **Take the quiz again** keeps both your best and your latest score.
- Under **Your progress in this module** you can undo any mark: remove the completed mark, clear the quiz score,
  or reset the flashcards.

**Example.** You finish a quiz with two questions wrong. Those two are kept for **Practice missed questions**, and
the module shows your score.

## Review flashcards

**What it does.** Brings back each flashcard just before you would forget it (spaced repetition), across all
modules.

**How to use it.**
1. On the home page, or under **Practice** in the top bar, choose **Review flashcards**.
2. Rate each card. The page counts how many are left in this session.

**Rules and limits.**
- A card you know comes back after 1 day, then 3, 7, 14 and 30 days.
- A card you miss comes back the same day.
- You can re-rate a single card at any time in its module.

**Example.** You rate "Team charter" as known today. It comes back tomorrow, then three days later.

## Practice missed questions

**What it does.** Collects every quiz and mock exam question whose latest answer was wrong, so you can practise
exactly those.

**How to use it.**
1. Open **Practice** in the top bar, then **Practice missed questions**.
2. Press **Practice all**, or choose one exam domain from the list.
3. Answer each question. At the end you see how many you now got right.

**Rules and limits.**
- A question leaves the list once you answer it correctly.
- Unanswered mock exam questions are not added.
- Clearing a module's quiz score also clears its questions from this list.

**Example.** You got 3 questions wrong in module 1.3. Practice shows 3 to practise; you answer 2 correctly, and 1
stays on the list.

## Mock exams

**What it does.** Full exams with the real number of questions, the real time and the real mix of exam domains.

**How to use it.**
1. Open **Practice** in the top bar, then the mock exams.
2. Choose an exam and press **Start in practice mode** (you see the answer after each question) or **Start in real
   exam mode** (no answers until the end, in sections with a break between them).
3. Flag questions to come back to. Press **Submit the exam and see the result** when you finish.
4. The result shows your score per domain and every question with its explanation.

**Rules and limits.**
- The clock starts when you start and keeps running when you close the page. You can close the page and continue
  later, but the time keeps passing.
- When the time is up, the exam is submitted with the answers given so far.
- At 30 and at 5 minutes left, a quiet reminder appears under the clock for a few seconds (**Hide** closes it).
- The break in real exam mode does not use exam time and ends by itself.
- **Stop without a result** discards that run.
- No question appears in two mock exams.
- One mock exam of each course is **kept for last** (CAPM 5, PMP 4): its questions are in no module quiz, so it shows
  how you do on questions you have never met.
- PMP mock exams include case sets (three questions on one situation, never split by a break), and the result also
  shows your score on predictive questions against agile and hybrid ones.
- To try the real exam's other question types first (CAPM: matching, hot spot, comic strip; PMP: multiple response,
  matching, hot spot, pull-down list, case set, graphic-based), use **Try one of each** on the mock exams page.

**Example.** The PMP mock exam has 180 questions in 240 minutes, in 3 sections with a 10-minute break after the
first two.

## Passed a certification

**What it does.** Once you have passed an exam, you can mark it, so its certification stops appearing on your study
pages while you work on the next one.

**How to use it.**
1. Open the certification's page (for example **CAPM** under **Your certification path** on the home page).
2. Press **I passed this exam**, then **Mark as passed**.
3. To bring it back, open the same page and press **Not passed yet: show it again**.

**Rules and limits.**
- Nothing is marked until you press the button: a certification is never hidden by itself.
- A marked certification is left out of the home page, the study plan and the dashboard tabs. Its modules, quizzes,
  mock exams and all your progress stay, and its page and lessons open as before.
- The mark is part of your progress, so it is synced and kept in backups.

**Example.** After you pass CAPM, mark it as passed: the home page then shows only PMP, and the certification path
reads "Passed" under CAPM.

## Study plan

**What it does.** Spreads the modules you have not finished over the days before your exam, and adjusts itself
every day.

**How to use it.**
1. Open **Study plan** in the top bar.
2. Enter your **Exam day**: type it (for example 3 Jan 2027) or press the calendar button beside the box.
3. The schedule shows what to study each day, and the days kept for final review.

**Rules and limits.**
- The plan stores only the exam day. Everything else is worked out fresh each day, so a missed day spreads over the
  days left and nothing piles up.
- A quarter of the time before the exam, at most 7 days, is kept for final review.
- Modules not written yet are included, so the pace is realistic.
- **Remove the date** removes the plan; your progress stays.

**Example.** With 40 days to go, the last 7 days are final review, and the other 33 days share the open modules.

## Dashboard

**What it does.** Shows your progress and quiz average per exam domain, your milestones, the questions to
practise and the modules to revisit.

**How to use it.**
1. Open **Dashboard** in the top bar. It opens on the certification you are studying; the tabs **CAPM** and **PMP**
   switch between them.
2. Read **Progress by exam domain** to see where you are weakest.
3. Use **Practice these questions** or **Modules to revisit** to work on that domain.

**Rules and limits.**
- The **Readiness estimate** uses your quizzes and mock exams. It is a guide, not a promise of the exam result.
- Quizzes taken before 10 Oct 2026 kept their scores but have no answer history.

**Example.** If one domain has the lowest quiz average, start with its modules under **Modules to revisit**.

## Formulas and key facts

**What it does.** Everything worth knowing by heart, on one page. Each line repeats what a lesson teaches and links
to it.

**How to use it.**
1. Open **Formulas** in the top bar.
2. Choose a section on the right, or press **Print this sheet**.

**Rules and limits.**
- Nothing on the sheet is new: each line comes from a lesson.
- CAPM and PMP each have their own sheet. **Formulas** opens the sheet of the certification you are in.
- The PMP sheet follows the Eighth Edition: 6 principles, 7 performance domains, 5 focus areas and 40 processes.

**Example.** "Cost performance index" in the PMP sheet shows CPI = EV ÷ AC, how to read it, a worked example
(80,000 ÷ 90,000 = 0.89) and a link to the lesson section where it is taught.

## Search

**What it does.** Finds where a term is taught, in the lessons and the flashcards of the certification you are in.

**How to use it.**
1. Type in the search box at the top (on a phone, press the magnifier first). Suggestions appear as you type.
2. Choose one to open that lesson section or flashcard, or press Enter to see every result.

**Rules and limits.**
- Quiz and exam questions are left out, so you never meet one here first.

**Example.** Searching "servant leadership" lists the lesson sections and flashcards that use the term.

## Sources and book links

**What it does.** Shows where each part of a lesson comes from: the book, the section and the page.

**How to use it.**
1. In a lesson, the source is written under each section heading.
2. In a quiz, each explanation names its source.
3. Click **Open the PDF at page …**. The **book reader** opens in a new tab, at that page:
   - on this PC (**start.bat**), with the book from your PMI Learning Material folder;
   - on the website, with your private online copy, once you are signed in (**Sign in**, at the top right).
4. In the reader, scroll through the book, type a page number and press Enter, or use **Previous page** and **Next
   page**. **−** and **+** make the pages smaller or larger; on a phone you can also pinch with two fingers.
5. **Search** finds words in the whole book: type them, press **Find**, and the arrows go from page to page; the
   words are marked in yellow. Select text on a page to copy it.
6. When you have moved away from the source page, the button with the arrow and its number takes you back.

**Rules and limits.**
- On the website, any signed-in account can open the books. Signed out, a link opens a small window, **Sign in
  first**, with a **Sign in** button.
- Opening a book without a page (for example from your browser's history) continues where you last read it.
- The first time a book opens on a device, the whole book downloads. Each link stays valid for 7 days on that
  device, so opening the same book again within the week costs nothing extra.
- **Monthly stop:** the online books stop at 4 GB of downloads a month, 1 GB under the free plan's 5 GB, so Supabase
  never restricts the project (sync included). From 3 GB, opening a book shows the month's total. At 4 GB the links
  pause until the 1st of the next month; books opened in the last 7 days still open, and on this PC every book opens
  as usual.
- "PDF page" is the page number your PDF viewer shows, not the number printed on the page.

**Example.** In module 2.10, "Variances and indices" shows "Source: PMBOK 8, Guide Table 5-1". Clicking **Open the
PDF at page 312** opens the PMBOK Guide at that table, on this PC or, signed in, on your phone.

## Your saved progress: backup and restore

**What it does.** Saves your progress automatically in the browser, and lets you keep or move a copy as a file.

**How to use it.**
1. On the home page, under **Your saved progress**, press **Download backup** to save a copy.
2. On another device or browser, press **Restore from backup** and choose that file.

**Rules and limits.**
- Progress is saved the moment you click, and stays when you close the browser or shut down the PC.
- Progress belongs to one browser on one device and one address: this PC, the website and your phone each have
  their own until you restore a backup or use sync.
- It is lost only if you clear the browser's site data, or use a private window.
- **Delete all progress** asks first, and cannot be undone. Download a backup before using it.

**Example.** Before you reinstall your browser, press **Download backup**. Afterwards, **Restore from backup** brings
everything back.

## Sign in and sync between your devices

**What it does.** Keeps your progress the same on your PC and your phone, through a free online account, and lets
you open the books on the website.

**How to use it.**
1. Press **Sign in** at the top right of any page (on a phone: the person icon). Enter your email address and, under
   **Email me a link**, press **Send the sign-in email**. A new email creates your account.
2. Open the link in the email on that device, or type the code from the email and press **Sign in**.
3. On a device that is signed in, open **Account** and save a password under **Password for your other devices**.
4. On every other device: **Sign in**, then **Use my password**, your email and the password.

**Rules and limits.**
- Until you sign in, nothing leaves your browser.
- Progress uploads about 4 seconds after a change, and downloads when you open the site. **Sync now** does both.
- If both devices changed while apart, both are kept; where both changed the same thing, the later change wins.
- The free email service sends only a few emails an hour. If one does not arrive, wait before asking again.
- The free online account is paused after about a week without use. Studying most days keeps it awake.
- Theme and text size are kept per device and are not synced.
- Signed in, the button at the top right reads **Account** and opens **Sync now**, the password and **Sign out on
  this device**. A red dot on it means the last sync failed; open it to read why.
- **Sign out on this device** stops syncing and leaves your progress on the device.

**Example.** You answer a quiz on your phone on the train. At home you open the site on your PC and the result is
already there.

## Text size and dark theme

**What it does.** Makes the text larger and switches between a light and a dark screen.

**How to use it.**
1. At the top right, press one of the three **A** buttons: normal, large or larger text. On a phone, press the
   half-filled circle first to show them.
2. Press **Dark** or **Light** to switch the theme.

**Rules and limits.**
- Both choices are kept in this browser only.
- Until you choose a theme, the app follows your device's own setting.
- The switch fades softly over half a second, so the screen does not flash. If your device is set to reduce
  motion, it switches at once.

**Example.** For a long evening session, choose the larger **A** and **Dark**.

## Version number and what's new

**What it does.** Shows which version of the app you are using, and what changed in each version.

**How to use it.**
1. The version number is at the bottom of every page.
2. Press **What's new** there, or open **Help** in the top bar and choose the **What's new** tab.

**Rules and limits.**
- Each version gets one entry on the day it is released, newest first.
- The short code after the version number (the build) tells which saved change the version was made from. It helps
  to check that the website runs the latest version.

**Example.** "Version 1.0 · build 1a2b3c4" at the bottom of the page means you are using version 1.0.
