# What's new

Each version of Road to PMP, newest first, with what you will notice.

## 1.1 · 24 Nov 2026 · More of the PMP Process domain

- **PMP modules 2.4 to 2.6:** Agile in Practice (Scrum, XP, Kanban, Lean and scaling), Scope (requirements, WBS and backlog), and Value-Based Delivery, with 90 questions and 75 flashcards.
- **PMP modules 2.7 to 2.9:** Resources, Procurement (contracts, negotiation, claims), and Finance (estimates, budget and reserves), with 90 questions, a new case set and 75 flashcards.
- **PMP modules 2.10 to 2.12:** Earned Value and Financial Reporting, Quality (cost of quality, audits, continuous improvement), and Schedule (dependencies, critical path, compression, baselines), with 90 questions, a new case set and 75 flashcards.
- **PMP modules 2.13 to 2.15:** Agile Scheduling (story points, velocity, release plans, flow metrics), Evaluating Status (metrics, measurement pitfalls, artifacts, dashboards), and Closure and Transition, with 90 questions, two new case sets and 75 flashcards. **The PMP Process domain is complete:** PMP now has 24 of 34 modules, 721 questions and 600 flashcards.
- **PMP Business Environment domain started:** 3.1 Governance, Ethics, Success Metrics and Escalation, 3.2 Compliance, 3.3 Change Control, with 90 questions, two new case sets and 75 flashcards. PMP now has 27 of 34 modules, 811 questions and 675 flashcards.
- Some PMP questions in 2.8 to 2.11 and 2.13 that were too close to CAPM questions were rewritten as new situations.

## 1.0 · 23 Nov 2026 · Help page, version number and the first PMP modules

- **Help** in the top bar: a guide to every part of the app, and this list of changes.
- The version number at the bottom of every page, with links to What's new and the guide.
- **PMP course started, with the whole People domain:** modules 1.1 to 1.9, 271 quiz questions and 225 flashcards. New today: 1.4 Team Performance, 1.5 Identifying and Analyzing Stakeholders, 1.6 Engaging Stakeholders, 1.7 Aligning and Managing Expectations, 1.8 Knowledge Transfer, 1.9 Communication and Reporting.
- **PMP Process domain started:** 2.1 The Framework, 2.2 Choosing the Approach, 2.3 The Integrated Project Management Plan, with 90 questions and 75 flashcards. PMP now has 12 modules, 361 questions and 300 flashcards.
- **Formulas** in the top bar now shows only for a certification that has a formula sheet.
- **PMP mock exams are ready for the real format:** questions with more than one right answer, pull-down lists,
  case sets (several questions on one situation), questions about a drawing, and the exam in 3 sections with
  breaks.
- Search now stays within the certification you are in.
- Already there from before: the complete CAPM course with 39 modules, 1,274 questions, 1,339 flashcards, 6 mock
  exams, the study plan, the dashboard, the formula sheet, backups and sync between devices.
