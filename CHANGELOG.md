# What's new

Each version of Road to PMP, newest first, with what you will notice.

## 1.1 · 24 Nov 2026 · The PMP course complete: modules, mock exams and formula sheet

- **Settings:** a new page in the top bar with everything you can set up: theme and text size, your account, the
  books on this device, backups of your progress, and certifications marked as passed. The backup buttons moved
  here from the bottom of the home page. **Delete all progress** now asks in a window of the app.
- **Books open faster on the website:** the first time a book opens on a device it is saved there, with a line
  showing how far it has got; after that it opens at once. **Save all books on this device** in Settings saves all
  19 (about 160 MB) in one go, and they can be removed again. Signing out removes them.

- **PMP formula sheet:** open **Formulas** on any PMP page for earned value, estimates and budget, the critical
  path, velocity and WSJF, communication channels, EMV and decision trees, and the Eighth Edition's principles,
  domains, focus areas and 40 processes. Each line links to its lesson.
- **Mock exams:** a quiet reminder at 30 and at 5 minutes left.
- **I passed this exam:** on a certification's page you can mark it as passed, so it leaves your home page, study
  plan and dashboard; its progress stays, and the mark can be taken off. Nothing is marked unless you press it.
- **Simpler sign-in:** one email box, with **Email me a link** or **Use my password**.
- **Sign in at the top right** of every page, where websites usually put it. Signed in, it reads **Account** and
  opens sync, the password and sign out. It is no longer at the bottom of the home page.
- **On a phone the top bar is one row:** the magnifier opens search, the half-filled circle opens text size and theme.
  Pages keep more of the screen, and the first visit loads faster.
- **Dashboard:** one certification at a time, with tabs for CAPM and PMP; it opens on the one you are studying.
- **Study plan:** the exam day shows as 3 Jan 2027 like every date in the app; type it or pick it from the calendar.
- **Book reader:** search inside a book, select and copy text, pinch to zoom on a phone, finer zoom steps, a button
  back to the source page, and the last page read is remembered per book.
- Windows such as **Sign in first** dim the page behind them more clearly in the dark theme.
- **Softer theme switch:** Dark and Light now fade into each other over half a second instead of flashing.
- **Book reader:** the top bar and the page controls stay at the top while you scroll through a book, so the menu
  and the page box are always at hand. On a phone the page controls fit on one row.
- **Book reader:** **Open the PDF at page …** now opens the book in the app's own reader, in a new tab, at that
  page, on your PC and on the website alike, also on your phone. Turn pages, type a page number, or make the pages
  larger. No more downloads instead of a book.
- **Book links on the website:** once you are signed in, the books open on the website too, from a private online
  copy (after a one-time setup in Supabase). Signed out, a link opens a small window asking you to sign in first.
- Module 2.9 now shows both published ranges for later cost estimates (−5% to +10%, and −10% to +15%) with their
  books.

- **PMP mock exams:** four full exams of 180 questions in 4 hours, in 3 sections with two breaks, each with case
  sets and every module of each domain. Mock exam 4 is kept for last: its questions are in no module quiz.
  The PMP mock exams appear in your study plan, the readiness estimate and the weak spots on the dashboard.
- The study plan now shows each mock exam's real length (PMP: 4 hours).
- Each PMP mock exam now has the same mix of question types (5 matching, 5 pull-down list, about 18 multiple
  response).
- Dates of quizzes and mock exams are written the same way everywhere, for example 20 Nov 2026.

- **PMP modules 2.4 to 2.6:** Agile in Practice (Scrum, XP, Kanban, Lean and scaling), Scope (requirements, WBS and backlog), and Value-Based Delivery, with 90 questions and 75 flashcards.
- **PMP modules 2.7 to 2.9:** Resources, Procurement (contracts, negotiation, claims), and Finance (estimates, budget and reserves), with 90 questions, a new case set and 75 flashcards.
- **PMP modules 2.10 to 2.12:** Earned Value and Financial Reporting, Quality (cost of quality, audits, continuous improvement), and Schedule (dependencies, critical path, compression, baselines), with 90 questions, a new case set and 75 flashcards.
- **PMP modules 2.13 to 2.15:** Agile Scheduling (story points, velocity, release plans, flow metrics), Evaluating Status (metrics, measurement pitfalls, artifacts, dashboards), and Closure and Transition, with 90 questions, two new case sets and 75 flashcards. **The PMP Process domain is complete:** PMP now has 24 of 34 modules, 721 questions and 600 flashcards.
- **PMP Business Environment domain started:** 3.1 Governance, Ethics, Success Metrics and Escalation, 3.2 Compliance, 3.3 Change Control, with 90 questions, two new case sets and 75 flashcards. PMP now has 27 of 34 modules, 811 questions and 675 flashcards.
- **PMP modules 3.4 to 3.6:** Impediments and Issues, Risk: Identifying and Analyzing, Risk: Responses and Monitoring, with 90 questions, two new case sets and 75 flashcards.
- **PMP modules 3.7 to 3.10:** Continuous Improvement and Lessons Learned, Organizational Change and Culture, The External Business Environment, Artificial Intelligence in Projects, with 120 questions, two new case sets and 100 flashcards. **All 34 PMP modules are now written:** 1,021 questions and 850 flashcards.
- Some PMP questions in 2.8 to 2.11 and 2.13 that were too close to CAPM questions were rewritten as new situations.

## 1.0 · 23 Nov 2026 · Help page, version number and the first PMP modules

- **Help** in the top bar: a guide to every part of the app, and this list of changes.
- The version number at the bottom of every page, with links to What's new and the guide.

New today: 1.4 Team Performance, 1.5 Identifying and Analyzing Stakeholders, 1.6 Engaging Stakeholders, 1.7 Aligning and Managing Expectations, 1.8 Knowledge Transfer, 1.9 Communication and Reporting.

- **Formulas** in the top bar now shows only for a certification that has a formula sheet.
- **PMP mock exams are ready for the real format:** questions with more than one right answer, pull-down lists,
  case sets (several questions on one situation), questions about a drawing, and the exam in 3 sections with
  breaks.
- Search now stays within the certification you are in.
- Already there from before: the complete CAPM course with 39 modules, 1,274 questions, 1,339 flashcards, 6 mock
  exams, the study plan, the dashboard, the formula sheet, backups and sync between devices.
